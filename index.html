About

Soloist

Italian pianist, Paris-based. My work lives in luxury hotels, fine dining and exclusive events: I’ve held solo residencies in prestigious venues in Paris and London, and played galas, brand events and private parties where the music had to be as refined as the setting. I’m also an official pianist at Disneyland Paris.

Music coordinator

I also coordinate musicians, from duos to full line-ups, for venues that want live music done right, week after week.
